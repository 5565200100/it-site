Optimization and Automation of Production and Logistics Processes

I help companies find and eliminate losses in production and logistics processes: I audit current workflows, identify bottlenecks and implement solutions that automate routine work and make use of artificial intelligence. The result is measurable: less manual work, faster order and document handling, transparent data for decision-making.

What I can do

  • Process audit: workflow and data maps, bottlenecks, sources of wasted time and errors
  • Digitizing document workflows: OCR recognition, classification and automatic routing of documents by type and owner
  • Automation of routine operations and integration with accounting systems (1C, ERP, WMS) via APIs
  • AI components: text and image processing, demand and workload forecasting, anomaly detection
  • Before/after effect evaluation, payback estimates, pilot launch at one site with subsequent scaling
  • Staff training and post-implementation support

Technologies: Python, AI provider APIs (OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter), OCR and data processing, integrations with 1C / ERP / WMS
