Privacy Policy

English · Effective date: September 11, 2026 / 11 сентября 2026 г.

This combined policy applies to this website and PDFSort, a PDF organizer for iPhone and iPad. The sections below describe website data processing and app data processing separately.

Website

When you use this website, data required to keep it functioning is exchanged between your computer or other user device and the server. This may include your IP address and browser name. Cookies may also be used to store and maintain a session and to retain temporary information.

We do not pass the collected information to third parties and do not collect personal data from you through this website. The exception is the Google Analytics 4 web analytics service (Google Ireland Limited). It records anonymised information about your browser, device, traffic source and pages visited so that we can improve the website. The IP address is truncated (anonymised) before it is stored. Details: Google's privacy policy.

1. General information

Protecting your personal data is especially important to us. We process data solely on the basis of applicable legal provisions, in particular the GDPR/DSGVO and TKG 2003. This privacy policy informs you about the most important aspects of data processing on our website.

2. Data collection when visiting the website

When you visit our website, certain technical data is exchanged between your device and our server. This is technically necessary to display the website correctly. The data collected includes:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system used
  • Date and time of access
  • Websites from which you reached us
  • Pages visited on our website

PDFSort app

3. On-device processing

Your PDF documents, search index, OCR results and settings are stored and processed locally in the app's MyPDF library on your device. By default, document classification happens entirely on your device. We do not receive, store or process your documents on our servers.

4. No tracking, no ads

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking and no data brokers. We do not collect personal data for marketing purposes.

5. Optional AI classification

If you choose to connect your own AI provider (OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter) in Settings, the file name and extracted text of your PDFs are sent over HTTPS to the selected provider's servers solely to classify the document. This happens only after you enter your own API key and activate the provider. Providers may retain requests for a short period in line with their own policies, for example for abuse monitoring. We never receive your PDF content or your API keys. API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. In App Store Connect this is disclosed as “Other User Content”.

6. iCloud

If you enable iCloud sync, your library is stored in your personal iCloud container and synchronized across your devices by Apple. Apple's iCloud privacy policy applies.

7. Donations and support

Donation links (PayPal, Ko-fi, cryptocurrency wallets) and the support email open in the system browser or mail app outside PDFSort. Payment and support details are handled by the respective providers and are not collected by the app.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

For questions about privacy, contact: extraort@gmail.com
