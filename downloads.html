PDFSort

PDFSort is a free alternative to Paperless for secure local use, developed by a German software developer. It reads the content of every document and automatically files it into categories and subcategories: Finance, Legal, Medical, Education, Personal, Work, Travel, Books and Insurance.

Search by file name and by the text inside documents, with full-text indexing and OCR for scanned copies. Classification works on-device using keyword analysis; for even greater accuracy you can optionally connect AI providers — OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter.

Your data stays on your device: no ads, no tracking and no mandatory accounts. Low-confidence and password-protected documents are flagged for review.
